01 / About

Research across communication, institutions and public life.

I am a researcher based in Astana, Kazakhstan. My primary interests include political communication and media pluralism, media governance and journalistic autonomy, and comparative communication in Central Asia.

My broader work also includes communication disorders and family communication, nuclear disarmament and global governance, and policy-relevant institutional research. I use qualitative, comparative, network, bibliometric and quantitative methods.
