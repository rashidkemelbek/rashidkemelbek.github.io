01 / About

Research across communication, institutions and public life.

I am a Kazakhstan-based researcher and communication professional whose work spans political communication, media systems, family and health communication, and global governance.

My primary research agenda examines how political and institutional conditions shape media pluralism, journalistic autonomy, and the adaptive capacity of independent media. Alongside this work, I develop research on childhood communication disorders and parental experiences, as well as nuclear disarmament, international security, and Kazakhstan’s position in global governance.

At the Kazakhstan Institute for Public Development (KIPD), I work as an Expert in the Department of Scientific and Methodological Support, contributing to qualitative and quantitative research, analytical frameworks, institutional projects, and scholarly publishing initiatives.
